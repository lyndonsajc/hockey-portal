Please read: Guidelines for claims

  1. Receipts must be submitted within seven days of the earliest receipt date. No late submissions will be entertained.
  2. Transport claims are valid only for journeys between School, a Training venue and Home.
  3. On a normal training day, the receipt time must not be later than 10:15 pm. Students should leave the venue by 8:30 pm, with allowance for dinner after training.
  4. Receipts must be clear and not smudged. No amendments may be made to receipts.
  5. Paste at most two receipts per clean page, using only stick glue.
  6. State the purpose of every claim clearly, including the starting point and destination. Include the exact address and venue name, for example “900 Tiong Bahru Road (Delta Hockey Pitch)” or “Potong Pasir Ave 3 (Home)”. Only School, Training venue and Home are allowed as location types.
  7. Write your name on every sheet and sign the first receipt at the bottom right.
  8. Grab, Uber and other alternative transport claims are accepted in addition to taxi claims, subject to the same guidelines.
  9. Teachers-in-Charge reserve the right to reject claims that do not meet these guidelines.

Grab receipt settings

In Grab, go to Account → Settings → Communications → GrabPay Communications, then turn on Transaction Statement. Menu labels may differ by app version.

Send a PDF of the GrabPay email receipt to the Teacher-in-Charge. The receipt must clearly show the trip's starting point and destination, including the address and venue name.
